## What was built

1. **"Next call" box** on the home page. The bottom row is now Community (2 columns), Next call
   (1) and Fair price & charts (1), with the play-button quarter faint behind Next call.
   - It takes its data from the same `GET /api/calls` the Calls page uses.
   - It shows a live call with "Live now" and Listen, else the next planned call (title, local
     day and time, its page), else a friendly line and "All calls".
2. **"Add to calendar"** for everyone, guests included: `GET /api/calls/:id/calendar.ics` for
   each planned call (RFC 5545).
   - Stable UID per call, DTSTAMP, start and end in UTC (start + 60 minutes, as calls have no
     planned end), title, description plus the call page's address, and a display alarm 15
     minutes before.
   - Text is escaped and long lines are folded.
   - Rate-limited per address (`callCalendar`), `allow('public')` as for the list.
   - The button sits in the Next call box and beside each planned call on the Calls page.
3. **"Remind me on this site"** for members: a toggle that stores a reminder (new table
   `call_reminders`).
   - The reminder goes when the member turns it off, when the call ends, or with the account
     or call.
   - Every open page of the member shows "<title> starts in 10 minutes" and "<title> is live
     now" with Listen and Dismiss.
   - The server pushes these over the chat's live connection (a personal event, no polling).
   - Each kind is shown once: it is marked only once it reached an open page, so a restart
     never repeats it. Dismissing in one tab dismisses it in all; nothing shows on that call's
     own page.
   - The job works like the stale-call job: at start, every 30 seconds, and at once when a call
     goes live.
   - Guests see neither the toggle nor any notice.
4. **Live Community box**: "● N online" and the newest 3 messages of "general" (name, short
   text, time), kept live.
   - It goes through the chat module's public `index.js` (`useRoomGlimpse`, `usePersonalEvents`);
     the module-boundary test stays green.
   - Messages come only while "general" may be read by everyone, and hidden messages never show.
   - Attachments show as `[image]`, `[GIF]` or `[file]`.
   - When the newest message is over 24 hours old, the box shows only the count and "Open the
     chat".
5. **Layout fixes**:
   - The Calls page shows a card for every empty list ("No call is planned yet — organisers
     announce them here", "No calls yet"), never "None.".
   - The prices page uses the wide column. From 1100px the fair price table and the converter
     stand side by side; below that they stack, with no sideways scroll.

**Reused**:

## Decisions taken by the task's rules

- The site had **no general on-site notification mechanism**. The notices therefore use the
  shell's banner style and the chat's personal-event push, as the docs say (docs/CALLS.md,
  "Reminders").
- **"Once per kind" is kept by the server**: a kind is marked delivered only when at least one
  open page received it. A member who opens the site inside the 10 minutes still gets it, at
  the next round (within 30 seconds).
- **The Community box asks its own small route** (`GET /api/chat/glimpse`), so the server alone
  guarantees "never members-only, never hidden". The client cannot judge a room's access.
- **The online count** is pushed as a number only, to the readers of "general".
- **When calls are not set up**, the Next call box is not shown and Fair price takes its column.

## Noticed and left alone

- The Calls page writes dates in the browser's locale (`toLocaleString(undefined)`). A German
  browser therefore shows "22.10.2026, 20:30" beside English text. The new Next call box uses
  the shared English format of "Up next" instead. The Calls page is unchanged; it could follow.
- Dismissed notices leave a small mark per call and kind in browser storage
  (`se.callNotice.<call>.<kind>`). These marks are never cleaned up.
