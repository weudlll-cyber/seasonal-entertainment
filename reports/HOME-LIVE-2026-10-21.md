# SE-HOME-LIVE-1 — report (2026-10-21)

## What was built

1. **"Next call" box** on the home page. The bottom row is now Community (2 columns), Next call
   (1) and Fair price & charts (1), with the play-button quarter faint behind Next call.
   - It takes its data from the same `GET /api/calls` the Calls page uses.
   - It shows a live call with "Live now" and Listen, else the next planned call (title, local
     day and time, its page), else a friendly line and "All calls".
2. **"Add to calendar"** for everyone, guests included: `GET /api/calls/:id/calendar.ics` for
   each planned call (RFC 5545).
   - Stable UID per call, DTSTAMP, start and end in UTC (start + 60 minutes, as calls have no
     planned end), title, description plus the call page's address, and a display alarm 15
     minutes before.
   - Text is escaped and long lines are folded.
   - Rate-limited per address (`callCalendar`), `allow('public')` as for the list.
   - The button sits in the Next call box and beside each planned call on the Calls page.
3. **"Remind me on this site"** for members: a toggle that stores a reminder (new table
   `call_reminders`).
   - The reminder goes when the member turns it off, when the call ends, or with the account
     or call.
   - Every open page of the member shows "<title> starts in 10 minutes" and "<title> is live
     now" with Listen and Dismiss.
   - The server pushes these over the chat's live connection (a personal event, no polling).
   - Each kind is shown once: it is marked only once it reached an open page, so a restart
     never repeats it. Dismissing in one tab dismisses it in all; nothing shows on that call's
     own page.
   - The job works like the stale-call job: at start, every 30 seconds, and at once when a call
     goes live.
   - Guests see neither the toggle nor any notice.
4. **Live Community box**: "● N online" and the newest 3 messages of "general" (name, short
   text, time), kept live.
   - It goes through the chat module's public `index.js` (`useRoomGlimpse`, `usePersonalEvents`);
     the module-boundary test stays green.
   - Messages come only while "general" may be read by everyone, and hidden messages never show.
   - Attachments show as `[image]`, `[GIF]` or `[file]`.
   - When the newest message is over 24 hours old, the box shows only the count and "Open the
     chat".
5. **Layout fixes**:
   - The Calls page shows a card for every empty list ("No call is planned yet — organisers
     announce them here", "No calls yet"), never "None.".
   - The prices page uses the wide column. From 1100px the fair price table and the converter
     stand side by side; below that they stack, with no sideways scroll.

**Reused**:

- the chat hub's `sendToUser` and `onlineUserIds`;
- the module's one connection, its personal events and `joinRoom`;
- `makeLimiter`, `allow`, `callSteps` (`configured`, `loadCall`) and `selfOrigin`;
- the stale-call job's pattern;
- `formatDay` / `formatLocal`, the same day and time "Up next" shows;
- the home boxes' `Wash`;
- the `.banner` style;
- `readStored` / `writeStored`;
- the `.calls-none` cards.

**Moved out**: the bottom row's styles went into `homeBottomRow.css`, which took
`LandingPage.css` from 335 to 284 lines. The static Community box was removed from
`homeCards.jsx`.

## Decisions taken by the task's rules

- The site had **no general on-site notification mechanism**. The notices therefore use the
  shell's banner style and the chat's personal-event push, as the docs say (docs/CALLS.md,
  "Reminders").
- **"Once per kind" is kept by the server**: a kind is marked delivered only when at least one
  open page received it. A member who opens the site inside the 10 minutes still gets it, at
  the next round (within 30 seconds).
- **The Community box asks its own small route** (`GET /api/chat/glimpse`), so the server alone
  guarantees "never members-only, never hidden". The client cannot judge a room's access.
- **The online count** is pushed as a number only, to the readers of "general".
- **When calls are not set up**, the Next call box is not shown and Fair price takes its column.

## Follow-up 2 (owner decision 2026-10-21)

### 1. Home fits the window again

The rule is again: from 1000px wide, home fits the window without scrolling from 1280 x 720 on
(owner decision 2026-10-17, confirmed 2026-10-21). This also holds at its fullest: the
58-character call, a signed-in member's two call buttons and 3 Community messages. No box is
cut, and nothing a box shows lies under the chat button.

With the window fit back, 1280 x 720 overflowed: Next call by 94px, "Up next" by 38px,
Featured by 10px. At 1440 x 900, Next call overflowed by 37px. **All four steps were needed**,
in order:

- **a)** A lower banner: 15 % of the window, 96–160px instead of 21 %, 120–190px. The lettering
  is capped by the window's height, so it scales down with the emblem; the greeting is
  unchanged. This also fixed Featured.
- **b)** Tighter spacing, from the tokens only: `--space-1` between the boxes and around the
  banner, `--space-2` / `--space-3` inside the boxes. After a and b: Next call 56px and "Up next"
  6px over at 1280 x 720; 1440 x 900 fit.
- **c)** "Add to calendar" and "Remind me" in one row. As text pills the two need 289px; the
  Next call box has 190px at 1280 x 720. Even the tightest padding with shortened words would
  have left a 1px margin. So in this box they are round icon buttons, calendar and bell. Their
  full names ("Add <title> to calendar", "Remind me of <title> on this site") are the
  accessible names, and "Add to calendar" / "Remind me on this site" the tooltips. The Calls page
  keeps the text buttons. After c: Next call 4px over.
- **d)** The Next call title clamped at 2 lines. The full title stays the link's name and
  tooltip.

Finishing touches:

- The top row now gets a little more than the bottom one (1.05 : 0.8 : 1.15 instead of
  1 : 0.8 : 1.2), for "Up next"'s two pictured events.
- In Community, "Open the chat" moved into the head row beside the online count. Otherwise the
  third message was hidden behind the button at 1280 x 720.

**Chat button**:

- The page-end space is gone.
- The bottom-right box (Fair price) keeps its content left of the button: its right padding is
  `--chat-launcher-size` plus `--space-1`.

**Browser test** (`client/e2e/layout.spec.js`, at 1280 x 720, 1440 x 900 and 1920 x 1080): "Home
fits without scrolling, no box cut, nothing under the chat button".

- It answers `/api/calls` and `/api/chat/glimpse` itself, with the long-titled call (dated 2000,
  so its date carries the year) and 3 recent messages. A first version made real data and failed

- "No box cut" now also compares each element with every clipping part inside the box. The first

### 2. One date format

Every date is now written as "Up next" wrote it: "Thursday 22 October, 20:30 CEST", English, 24
hours, the year only when it is not the current one. A non-breaking space keeps the zone with
its time.

- **One formatter**: `formatDateTime` in `shared/localWeek.js`, built from "Up next"'s
  `formatDay` and `formatLocal`.
- **In the browser**: `formatWhen` from `client/src/app/dates.js`, in the device's zone. That
  file's `deviceZone()` replaces three identical copies (in the home, events and prices pages).

**Replaced** (each place):

| Place                                                            | Before                                               | Now           |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | ------------- |
| Home, "Up next"                                                  | its own `formatDay` + `formatLocal`                  | `formatWhen`  |
| Home, Next call                                                  | its own `when()`                                     | `formatWhen`  |
| Calls page, "Planned for" / "Started" / "Ended"                  | `toLocaleString` in the browser's language           | `formatWhen`  |
| A call's page, "Started …"                                       | browser-language time only                           | the full date |
| Prices chart: axis, read-out, "% change since", the chart's name | `formatPriceTime`, "21 Oct 2026, 14:00"; now removed | `formatWhen`  |
| Admin, audit log ("When", no longer "(UTC)")                     | raw ISO text                                         | `formatWhen`  |
| Admin, chat sanctions "until …"                                  | ISO text in UTC                                      | `formatWhen`  |
| Admin, reported private conversations: shared copies             | ISO text in UTC                                      | `formatWhen`  |
| Admin, info pages' last change                                   | ISO date only                                        | `formatWhen`  |

**Unchanged**:

- time-only stamps: chat messages and the Community box ("20:27");
- the events page, whose day headings and time column already use this format, split into a
  heading and a column, with the UTC time in brackets;
- the converter's `toLocaleString('en-US')`, which formats numbers, not dates.

No `toLocaleString` for dates is left.

**Tests**:

- `shared/localWeek.test.js`: the format, and the year only when needed.
- `callsPage.test.jsx`: "dates are the site's English format, also in a German browser". Every
  date format without an explicit language is made German there. It went red once with the old
  `toLocaleString` code.
- **Seven existing tests** expected the old texts and now expect the shared format:
  - prices (3);
  - comparison chart (2);
  - admin audit;
  - admin direct reports;
  - "Up next".

  Where a page uses the device's zone, the expectation is built with the same helper, so it
  holds on every machine.
