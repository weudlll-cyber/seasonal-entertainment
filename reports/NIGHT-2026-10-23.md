- **Footer, every page**: "Not financial advice" beside Imprint and Privacy, in the sidebar and in
  the phone menu (one list, `LEGAL_NAV` in `client/src/app/navigation.js`). In the 248px sidebar
  the three links do not fit one line, so the footer wraps and the new link sits on a line of
  its own (`flex-wrap` on `.sidebar__legal`). Home still fits 1280 x 720 without scrolling.
- **Disclaimer page** (`server/src/info/seed/disclaimer.md`, slug `disclaimer`): the points asked
  for, in plain English, with the official disclaimer as its source. Seeded published like the
  ten content pages (after FAQ, before Imprint).
- **The notice** "Information only — not financial advice." with a "Disclaimer" link
  (`client/src/components/NotAdvice.jsx`): on the prices page right above the fair price and the
  converter, and inside the home fair-price box. The wording and the address are in
  `shared/disclaimer.js`, so the weekly recap (piece 3) will use the same sentence.
- **The home box**: it was one big link, and a link cannot hold another. It is now a section
  whose title link covers the whole box (`.home__fair-link::after`), so it stays one click
  target; the notice sits above that cover and keeps its own Disclaimer link.
- **Dev database**: the Disclaimer created and published through the admin API as devadmin
  (audited `info.create`, `info.publish`), then put after FAQ by the admin reorder, as in the
  seed. Imprint and Privacy stay drafts.
- **Reused**: `LEGAL_NAV` and the sidebar footer, `readSeedPage` / the seed, the admin info
  routes, the shared package's exports.
