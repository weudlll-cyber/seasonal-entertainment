## Piece 2 — Season clock on home

- **Source of truth**: the token contracts (github.com/seasonaltokens/seasonaltokens,
  `contracts/*Token.sol`), as a pure module `shared/seasonClock.js`. Copied with file:line in
  its header: `INITIAL_REWARD` 168 / 140 / 120 / 105, `DURATION_OF_FIRST_ERA` 3/4, 3/2, 9/4 and 3
  years, `DURATION_OF_ERA` 3 x 365 days, `rewardEra` and the reward `INITIAL_REWARD / 2^era`.
- **Creation times** (decision rule: readable without a key, so real values): each contract's
  `contractCreationTime()` read with a read-only `eth_call` through a public JSON-RPC endpoint
  (ethereum-rpc.publicnode.com). The addresses were taken from the official site's page at run
  time by the script and never printed or stored; only the four timestamps were kept, all on
  2021-09-05 between 10:27:54 and 10:34:30 UTC. So no "about" marks: the halvings are shown
  to the day (never a time).
- **On home**: in the banner's free space on the right: the token in season (the highest
  reward now: its icon on a white disc ringed in its season colour), "Next halving: <token>,
  <date>" and "Calculated from the token contracts' halving schedule." Today: Autumn in season
  (reward 60, then Winter 52.5, Spring 42, Summer 35); next halving Autumn, Friday 4 December 2026. The banner keeps its height, and home fits 1280 x 720. On a phone the banner wraps and
  the clock takes a line of its own; the emblem and the lettering stay side by side.
- **The date by the day**: `formatDate` in `shared/localWeek.js`, the part of
  `formatDateTime` before the time, now used by both (one rule for the year); `formatDate` in
  `client/src/app/dates.js` for the viewer's zone.
- **The earlier disagreement, settled** (docs/BACKLOG.md): the contract schedule puts the
  second Spring halving on 5 June 2025 (UTC); the Medium update's 5 September 2025 is not the
  contract's date. Total supply: 33,112,800 per token (`SpringToken.sol:22`, the same in all
  four). The part-one entry is replaced by a part-two one.
- **Known halvings, from the module** (UTC): Spring 6 June 2022, Summer 6 March 2023, Autumn
  5 December 2023, Winter 4 September 2024, Spring 5 June 2025, Summer 5 March 2026 (6 March in
  Vienna); next Autumn 4 December 2026.
- **Reused**: `SEASON_ICONS`, `TOKENS`, the season colour tokens, `formatDay` / `yearOf` of
  `shared/localWeek.js`, the price tiles' white disc.
- **A slip of mine, corrected before the commit**: the first version of the client test
  expected Saturday 5 December and Winter in season a year later. The module was right: the
  contracts' 365-day years and 2024's leap day give 4 December, and by October 2027 Winter has
  halved too, so Spring is in season.
