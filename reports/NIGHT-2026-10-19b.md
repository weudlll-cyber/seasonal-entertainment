### Part 1 — live room message right after login

**Answer: yes, it could miss its live update until reload**, in two ways. Both are held open
deterministically in client/src/chat/chatCatchUp.test.jsx, and both were red against the old
hook:

- (a) A message stored after the panel's load but before the server has taken the new socket's
  subscription (the socket reopens as the new reader right after login) was in neither the
  load nor a push.
- (b) A message pushed while the load after the login was still on its way was wiped when the
  load landed (the load replaced the list).

**Fix**: on every `subscribed` from the server, the panel fetches
`GET /api/chat/rooms/<room>/messages?after=<newest id it has>` (the room's normal read rule
applies), and a load or catch-up is merged by id into the list instead of replacing it. A
stale load or catch-up for a room or reader left meanwhile is dropped. No timeout changed.
Backlog item closed.
