## NEEDS OWNER'S WORD

- **Age confirmation is not stored.** Registration requires the "at least 14" checkbox, but
  nothing about it is kept (no date of birth, as decided, and also no "confirmed at" timestamp).
  If you want proof that each account confirmed it, a timestamp column is a small change.
- **Email addresses are not verified.** No mail is sent; an address is only shape-checked. A
  confirmation mail (and password reset by mail) needs a mail provider you choose.
- **CSRF strict by default** (`CSRF_STRICT=true`): any state-changing API call without an Origin
  header is refused, also in development. Stricter than the race game; safe default.

- **TOTP secrets are stored unencrypted** in the database (the recovery codes are hashed).
  Encrypting the secrets needs a key kept outside the database (an environment variable, and a
  plan for rotating it); not done without your word.
- **Dev admin with 2FA:** the requirement stays ON. The dev admin's 2FA was turned on through
  the real API; its TOTP secret, `otpauth://` link and recovery codes are in
  `data/DEV-ADMIN.txt` (gitignored) next to the password. Add the secret to an authenticator
  app before logging in.
