## PIECE 4 — own room message "seen twice" in the chat on another site

Commit `2764325`. It took about 25 minutes, well within the 90.

**Result: the cause is found. The message was never shown twice; my browser check looked in the
wrong place.**

- **The code path**: every way into a room's list keeps one entry per message id.
  - The live push, the send's own answer and the catch-up after `subscribed` all go through
    `applyEvent` (a push of a known id is ignored) or `mergeMessages` (a Map by id).
  - The server sends the same object, with a numeric id, to the room push and to the sender's
    answer.
  - So the list cannot hold one message twice.
- **What `getByText` found**:
  - React keeps a controlled `<textarea>`'s `defaultValue`, and so its text content, equal to
    the draft.
  - The composer clears its field only once the server has answered the send, so a refused
    message is not lost.
  - The live push often arrives before that answer. For that moment the text is in the list
    **and** in the field, and an unscoped `getByText(text)` matches both.
- **Reproduced**:
  - **Deterministic**: the room message POST's answer held back for 1.5 s in the browser. In 3
    of 3 runs the matches were `span in list` and `textarea`; the list held it once, and once
    after the answer (`16-p4-text-twice-held.png`).
  - **Natural**: 30 runs of the SE-EMBED-CHECK scenario-1 step, a member on the foreign-site
    embed sending a room message.
    - In 2 of 30 runs (10 and 24) the text was found twice right after Send: list + textarea
      (`17-p4-text-twice-natural.png`).
    - In 11 runs the wait was satisfied by the **textarea alone**, before the message had even
      arrived.
    - Never twice in the list; never twice after the answer.
    - Paced at about 2 s per run: members may send 5 messages in 10 seconds.
- **The same blind spot in this repository's tests**:
  - Three client tests checked a just-sent message with an unscoped `findByText`:
    - `chatPanel.test.jsx` "shows its room and sends";
    - `chatComposer.test.jsx` (emoji);
    - `pages/chatPages.test.jsx` (guest send).
  - With `send()` sabotaged so the message is never listed, all three **still passed**: they
    found the textarea.
  - They now look inside the message list. With the same sabotage, all three are red.
  - The e2e specs were already scoped (to the list, a list item or a fresh page), or send through
    the API.
- **Pinned by a new test** (`chatPanel.test.jsx`): the sender's own message pushed before the
  send is answered. It is listed once, the field still holds the text, and after the answer the
  field clears and the message is still listed once.
  - Proven red twice: by appending without the id check (listed twice), and by clearing the
    field before the answer.
- **Docs**:
  - docs/CHAT.md: a new "Sending" bullet with the field-until-answer rule, the once-per-id list,
    and how tests should look for a just-sent message.
  - docs/BACKLOG.md: the item moved from part one to part two, with what closed it.
