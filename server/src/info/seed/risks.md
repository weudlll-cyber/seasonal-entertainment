# Risks

This page is not financial advice. The official disclaimer says: "Nothing on this website constitutes investment advice, a promise of any kind, or a guarantee of future prices or performance."

## Price and trading risks

- You can make a loss by buying tokens and then selling them at a lower price.
- Future token prices in dollars cannot be guaranteed or predicted.
- Trading tokens for more tokens can raise the number of tokens you own. It does not mean their dollar value will rise.
- The price cycles depend on a model of how markets react to changes in supply. Future prices may behave differently.
- The risk of a trading loss measured in dollars cannot be eliminated. Only the risk of a loss measured in tokens can be eliminated.

## Practical risks

The info sheet lists these: "Prices can move unexpectedly. Bad timing may reduce token gains. Gas fees or wallet errors can cause losses."

## Software risks

The smart contracts were audited, but the disclaimer says there is no way to fully guarantee that they, or software that works with them, are free of vulnerabilities. The contracts cannot be changed once deployed.

## No one is responsible for your losses

Seasonal Tokens is a decentralized project with no central authority. Token holders have no contract with the website maintainers. The maintainers accept no responsibility for losses, including losses from exploits or trading.

## Do your own research

The disclaimer asks people to do their own research and to consult independent legal, financial or other professional advisors before making decisions.
