# How it works

## The four seasons

There are four tokens: Spring, Summer, Autumn and Winter. When a token is "in season", it is produced at the fastest rate of the four. After its halving, it goes out of season and is produced at the slowest rate. The sources say this scarcity is expected to push that token's price up compared with the others: Spring, then Summer, then Autumn, then Winter, then Spring again. Four months after a token goes out of season, the farm switches from paying out the least for it to paying out the most.

## Halvings: the schedule

- The technical whitepaper says each token's reward "halves every three years, with a nine month interval between the halving of each token and the halving of the next token".
- The info sheet puts it this way: "Every nine months, one token's mining output is cut in half."
- Halvings happen at fixed scheduled times, not after a set number of tokens has been mined.
- Past halvings listed on the official site: Spring in June 2022, Summer in March 2023 (6 March 2023), Autumn in December 2023 and Winter in September 2024.
- The home page's production table also lists a Spring halving in June 2025 and a Summer halving in March 2026.

## The token-for-token strategy

The sources describe one rule: "always trade tokens for more tokens of a different type". For example, trade 3 Spring tokens for 5 Summer tokens. If you follow this rule, the total number of tokens you hold goes up with every trade. The sources describe holding a token for about nine months, then trading it for the next token in the cycle.

This rule protects only the token count. The value in dollars can still fall, and future prices may not follow the model. This is not financial advice.
