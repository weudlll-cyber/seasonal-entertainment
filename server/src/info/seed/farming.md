# Farming

Farming lets token holders and miners earn a regular income of tokens. You provide liquidity on Uniswap and then deposit that liquidity position into the Seasonal Tokens farm. The farm is a smart contract.

## Why the farm exists

The farm helps make sure there is enough liquidity for trades. It also creates a rotating demand for the tokens. According to the official site, 9% of tokens mined are distributed to farmers. Farmers receive Spring, Summer, Autumn and Winter tokens for as long as their liquidity stays in place.

## Steps (as described on the farming page)

1. Choose a token. The farm summary shows the ETH in each trading pair. The pair with the biggest shortfall of ETH, compared with the ratio 5:6:7:8 (Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter), is the most profitable to farm.
2. Get roughly equal values of ETH and that token.
3. Add liquidity on Uniswap with a v3 position. The farm does not work with v4 positions. Select "Full Range".
4. Deposit the position on the [farming page](https://seasonaltokens.org/farming). It is then locked for 30 days.
5. Harvest your farmed tokens at any time.

For the fee tier, the farming page FAQ says 1% on Ethereum and 0.01% on Polygon. Please check the farming page before you start. The community farming guide suggests Polygon for lower gas fees.

## Lock and withdrawal

Rewards are shared in proportion to the liquidity each farmer provides. Withdrawals are locked for 30 days. After withdrawals become available, you have 7 days to withdraw before the position is locked for another 30 days. The lock helps keep liquidity available, even in a panic.

Adding liquidity can cost a lot of gas. For token contract addresses, please use the official site: [seasonaltokens.org](https://seasonaltokens.org/).
