# FAQ

## What are Seasonal Tokens?

Four Proof-of-Work tokens: Spring, Summer, Autumn and Winter. They are designed so that their prices cycle around each other over the course of years.

## How often do halvings happen?

Each token's mining reward halves every three years. There is a nine-month gap between one token's halving and the next token's halving. So one of the four tokens halves every nine months.

## What is the main trading rule?

"Always trade tokens for more tokens of a different type." This makes your total number of tokens go up with every trade. It does not protect the value in dollars.

## Will the dollar price go up?

There is no guarantee of that. The official site says the supply rules drive the prices of the tokens relative to each other, not relative to the dollar. This is not financial advice.

## Did the founders get free tokens?

No. The official FAQ says the founders need to buy or mine tokens like everybody else.

## Has the code been audited?

Yes. The official site links to three audits: Callisto Security, QuillAudits and Paladin Blockchain Security. See our [security audits page](/info/security-audits) for the links.

## Where can I buy the tokens?

The official FAQ says the tokens can be traded for ETH on Uniswap, using the official [trade page](https://seasonaltokens.org/trade). You can also add the tokens to MetaMask and use its swap feature. For contract addresses, please use the official site: [seasonaltokens.org](https://seasonaltokens.org/).

## Can the smart contracts be changed?

No. The official site says the smart contracts cannot be changed or modified once deployed.
