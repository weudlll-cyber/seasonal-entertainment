# Charts and trading

This community page does not show prices or market data. For up-to-date information, please use the official pages linked below.

## Charts

The official charts page shows how the token prices have behaved. It also shows theoretical prices. The official FAQ explains that these theoretical prices assume the market will react to future halvings at the same rate as it did in the past. There is no guarantee that prices will behave this way.

The theoretical prices assume that each token's price moves about 1% a day towards the value given by its production rate. This is how the market reacted to the Spring halving in June 2022. The official site says the chart is hypothetical and should not be used to decide when or how to trade.

The live price charts on the official charts page come from LiveCoinWatch.

- [Seasonal Tokens charts](https://seasonaltokens.org/charts)

## Trading

The official trade page lets you trade the tokens for ETH on Uniswap. The official site also says the tokens can be traded on Ethereum and on Polygon.

When trading one Seasonal Token for another, the official site describes this rule: "Always trade tokens for more tokens of a different type." This keeps your total token count going up with each trade. It does not protect the value in dollars.

The official trade page gives some advice for trading:

- For trades worth more than about $100, it recommends turning on "smart transactions" in MetaMask, because front-running bots have been seen.
- A large single trade gets a worse price than the one quoted (this is called slippage). Check the amounts in MetaMask or Uniswap before you confirm.
- Buying in small amounts regularly gets you more tokens than one large purchase.

- [Seasonal Tokens trade page](https://seasonaltokens.org/trade)

Trading carries a risk of loss. Nothing here is financial advice.
