# What are Seasonal Tokens

Seasonal Tokens are four cryptocurrencies named Spring, Summer, Autumn and Winter. They are mined using Proof-of-Work. The project describes them as "an ethical and non-speculative tool for building wealth in the form of digital assets (tokens)".

## The basic idea

The tokens are designed so that their prices cycle around each other over the course of years. A user can trade the more expensive token for a larger number of a cheaper one. The aim is to grow the number of tokens a user holds over time, rather than to bet on the dollar price.

The project states clearly that this does not remove the risk of a loss measured in dollars. Only the risk of a loss measured in tokens can be removed.

## Technical basics

- The tokens are ERC20 tokens on the Ethereum blockchain. They also follow the ERC-918 standard for mineable tokens.
- The tokens can be bought and traded on Ethereum and on Polygon.
- The smart contracts cannot be changed once deployed.
- Mining began on 5 September 2021.

## Fair distribution

New tokens can only be produced by mining. The official FAQ says no tokens were given to the founders. They must buy or mine tokens like everyone else. The info sheet also lists "no pre-mine or team allocation".
