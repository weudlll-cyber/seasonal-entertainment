### Needs the owner's word

- **The home box votes in place only for single-choice polls.** The brief asked for voting in
  the card for single and multiple choice; checkboxes for several answers do not fit the top
  row at 1280 x 720 (the layout test failed with them), so a multiple-choice poll shows a

- **Sidebar pills 40px tall in short windows.** The new "Polls" item made the fullest staff menu

  of window height the pills are now 40px instead of 44px (DESIGN.md said they keep 44px), and
  the menu fits again with no scrollbar. Other ways: let it scroll there, or move Polls
  elsewhere.
- **Anonymous polls keep the first vote.** "Changing a vote until the end" (results after the
  end) cannot work in an anonymous poll without storing which ballot is whose, which the
  anonymity rule forbids ("store only what is needed for one vote per account"). Decided
  unattended: anonymity wins, the vote is final there, and the create form says so. The
  alternative would be a keyed hash of account and poll on the ballot (linkable by anyone with
  the server's secret).
- **An account deletion and anonymous ballots.** For the same reason, a deleted account's
  ballot in an anonymous poll cannot be found and stays (its "voted" mark goes). Its written
  answer stays too.
