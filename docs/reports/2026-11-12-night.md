- **Piece 2 — share card time zone** (`feat/recording-share`, b9c6357). The recap's zone
  (Europe/Vienna, `RECAP_ZONE`) became `SITE_ZONE` in `shared/localWeek.js`, used by the recap
  and now by the share card: "Tuesday 10 November, 19:00 CET" with the zone's abbreviation, as

  Full verify green (server 770, client 612, browser 177).
  - What the owner will see: a shared recording's card shows Vienna time with "CET"/"CEST".
- **Piece 3 — recordings backed up once** (`feat/backup-recordings`, 89ee71b). The nightly
  archive leaves `media/recordings` out (recordings live there in production, so until now every
  recording was in every nightly archive). Right after it, on the same timer, every finished
  recording without a backup is encrypted once to the same key into
  `/var/backups/seasonal/recordings/<file>.age`; one still recording waits for the next night;
  one changed after its backup (the egress finishing late) is backed up once more; one deleted
  on the site keeps its backup 14 days (`.gone` mark). So every recording is in a backup after
  the first nightly run after it ended: no gap. The PC scripts (PowerShell module and sh) fetch
  the recordings' backups they do not have, by name and size, into `recordings\`, and never
  delete them. Restore: `restore.mjs --recordings <folder>` puts them back and names every
  recording without a file ("MISSING", exit code 1). Tests: server (archive without recordings,
  once, waiting, again, 14 days, plain copy, restore with missing), the sh test with the fake
  sftp, the PowerShell tests (7 and 5.1); breaks in each went red. Full verify green.
  - What the owner will see: a `recordings` folder beside the nightly archives on the server and
    in the PC's backup folder; much smaller nightly archives; the restore drill in
    DEPLOYMENT.md has a step for the recordings.
